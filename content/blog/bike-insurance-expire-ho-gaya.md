## 1. Sabse Pehle: Gaadi Road Par Mat Nikaliye

Bina valid motor insurance ke bike chalana **Motor Vehicles Act 2019 Section 196** ke tehat gair-kanooni hai:
* **Pehli baar pakde gaye:** ₹2,000 ka court challan ya 3 mahine tak ki jail.
* **Dusri baar pakde gaye:** ₹4,000 ka seedha fine.

Lekin fine se bhi bada risk ye hai ki agar road par kisi dusre insaan ya gaadi ko chot lag gayi, to **Third Party Liability Court (MACT)** ka saara harjana (jo lakhon mein ho sakta hai) aapki personal savings se vasoola jaayega.

---

## 2. 90-Din Ka Golden Rule: NCB Bachana Zaroori Hai

Ye sabse zaroori baat hai jo zyadatar log nahi jaante:

**No Claim Bonus (NCB)** wo reward discount hai jo har saal claim na lene par milta hai — **20% se lekar 50% tak**.

* **Expiry ke 90 din ke andar:** Agar aap 90 din ke andar policy renew kar lete hain, to aapka poora **NCB discount 100% safe rehta hai**.
* **90 din nikalne ke baad:** NCB poora zero ho jaata hai. Agle saal se premium par 20% se 50% extra kharcha aayega.

> **Example:** Agar aapki bike ka comprehensive premium ₹2,500 ban raha tha aur 50% NCB tha, to aapko sirf ₹1,250 dena padta. Agar 90 din ka gap hua, to seedha ₹2,500 jeb se jaayega!

## Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal (FAQ)

### Sawaal: Bike insurance expire hone ke kitne din baad tak NCB bachta hai?
Policy expire hone ke theek 90 calendar days tak aapka No Claim Bonus (NCB) safe rehta hai. 91st day par NCB zero ho jaata hai. Isliye 90 din se pehle policy zaroor renew kar lein.

### Sawaal: Agar bina insurance bike chalate pakde gaye to kitna challan katega?
Motor Vehicles Amendment Act ke Section 196 ke tehat pehli baar bina insurance gaadi chalane par ₹2,000 ka fine ya 3 mahine ki jail ho sakti hai, aur repeat offense par ₹4,000 ka fine lagta hai.
