## 1. Accident Spot Par Kya Karein? (Pehle 15 Minute)

1. **Safety First:** Pehle sabhi passengers aur khud ko safe spot par le jaaiye.
2. **Accident Spot Photos & Video:** Gaadi ko hilane se pehle door se aur close-up 3-4 photos aur 30 second ki video le lijiye jisme gaadi ka number plate aur damage area clearly dikhe.
3. **Third Party Involvement:** Agar kisi dusre ki gaadi se takkar hui hai, to shanti se unki gaadi ka number aur driving license photo note kar lijiye. Ladai jhagda karne se claim process delay ho sakti hai.

## 2. FIR Kab Zaroori Hai Aur Kab Nahi?

Bohot se log sochte hain ki har claim ke liye Police FIR compulsory hoti hai. Aisa bilkul nahi hai:

* **FIR KI ZAROORAT NAHI HAI (Direct Claim Pass):**
  * Gaadi khadi thi aur kisi ne scratch/dent maar diya.
  * Divider, pole ya deewar se takkar ho gayi.
  * Self-damage ya chhota accident bina kisi physical injury ke.
* **FIR COMPULSORY HAI:**
  * **Theft (Chori):** Car ya bike chori hone par 24 ghante ke andar FIR zaroori hai.
  * **Major Bodily Injury / Death:** Agar kisi teesre vyakti ko gambhir chot aayi ho.
  * **Hit and Run:** Jahan saamne wala gaadi chhodkar bhaag gaya ho.

## 3. Cashless Claim vs Reimbursement Claim

| Feature | Cashless Claim Settlement | Reimbursement Claim Settlement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Workshop Type** | Company ke Authorized Network Garage par | Non-network ya local garage par |
| **Payment Responsibility** | Insurance Company direct workshop ko payment karti hai | Pehle aapko poora bill apni jeb se bharna padta hai |
| **Aapko Kitna Dena Hota Hai?** | Sirf Compulsory Deductible (₹1,000 - ₹2,000) | Poora bill pehle aap dete hain, 15-30 din baad refund aata hai |
| **Time Frame** | Fast (2 se 4 din mein delivery) | Slow (Verification mein mahina lag sakta hai) |

SafeDrive hamesha recommend karta hai ki aap **Cashless Network Garage** hi choose karein.

## 4. Claim Settlement Ke Liye Zaroori Documents

Workshop me gaadi drop karte waqt ye documents sath rakhein:
1. **Duly Signed Claim Form** (SafeDrive aapko digital bhej deta hai).
2. **Gaadi ki RC (Original & Copy).**
3. **Driving License (DL):** Jo insaan accident ke waqt drive kar raha tha uska valid license.
4. **Active Insurance Policy Copy.**
5. **Estimate of Repair:** Jo workshop generate karega.

## Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal (FAQ)

### Sawaal: Car insurance claim pass hone mein kitne din lagte hain?
Cashless network garage par surveyor inspection 24 se 48 ghante ke andar ho jaati hai. Approval aane ke baad repairs complete hote hi insurance company direct garage ko settlement bhej deti hai. Aamtaur par minor damage 3-4 din mein clear ho jaata hai.

### Sawaal: Claim lene par No Claim Bonus (NCB) khatam ho jaata hai kya?
Haan, agar aapne policy year mein koi claim le liya hai, to agle saal ka NCB discount zero ho jaayega. Isliye agar nuksan chhota hai (jaise ₹1,500 ka bumper scratch), to claim na lekar NCB bachana zyada faydemand hota hai.
