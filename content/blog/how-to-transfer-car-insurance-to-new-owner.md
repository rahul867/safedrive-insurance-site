## 1. The 14-Day Golden Rule: Section 157 Of Motor Vehicles Act

**Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 ke Section 157** ke tehat, jab bhi kisi gaadi ka ownership transfer hota hai, naye khariddar (buyer) ko **14 calendar days** ke andar insurance company ko formal transfer application deni zaroori hai.

Lekin is 14 din ke niyam mein ek bohot bada technical trap hai jo 99% log nahi jaante:

| Policy Component | 14 Din Ka Status | Kya Accident Par Claim Milega? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Third-Party Cover** | Automatic Deemed Transfer | ✅ **HAAN** — Court liability cover rehti hai |
| **Own Damage (OD) Cover** | **NO Automatic Transfer** | ❌ **NAHI** — Insurable interest na hone par claim reject ho jaayega |

> **Crucial Warning:** Agar aapne second-hand car khareedi, gaadi ka accident 10th day par ho gaya aur RC aapke naam thi lekin insurance purane maalik ke naam par hi tha — to insurance company **Own Damage repair ka ₹1 bhi nahi degi!** Supreme Court aur Consumer Court ke clear rulings hain ki Own Damage ka benefit sirf usi ko milta hai jiska naam policy schedule par registered ho.

## 2. No Claim Bonus (NCB) Rule: Gaadi Ka Nahi, Driver Ka Reward

Sabse zyada confusion No Claim Bonus (NCB) ko lekar hota hai:

### Rule 1: NCB Transfer Nahi Hota (It Belongs to the Seller)
Under IRDAI and India Motor Tariff rules, **NCB driver ke safe driving record ka reward hai, vehicle ka nahi.** Purane maalik ne 5 saal bina claim ke gaadi chalai aur 50% discount kamaya tha, to wo discount naye buyer ko automatically nahi milega.

### Rule 2: Seller Ka Bada Fayda (NCB Retention Certificate)
Agar aap gaadi bech rahe hain, to aap apni insurance company ko inform karke **NCB Retention Certificate** le sakte hain:
* Ye certificate **3 saal tak valid** rehta hai.
* Jab bhi aap nayi gaadi khareedenge, aapko apni purani 20% se 50% NCB discount ka fayda seedha nayi gaadi par mil jaayega.

### Rule 3: Buyer Ko NCB Recovery Pay Karni Padti Hai (GR-17 Rule)
India Motor Tariff ke **General Regulation 17 (GR-17)** ke anusaar, jab insurance transfer hota hai, to policy par laga hua purana NCB discount khatam ho jaata hai. Naye buyer ko bache hue tenure ka **pro-rata NCB difference amount** insurance company ko pay karna padta hai.

> **Ganit Samajhiye (Example):**  
> Maan lijiye car ki annual policy mein 6 mahine bache hain. Seller ko saal ka ₹4,000 ka 50% NCB discount mila tha. Ab transfer karte waqt 6 mahine ka bacha hua pro-rata discount (₹2,000) + ₹50 nominal transfer fee buyer ko pay karni hogi. Iske baad hi endorsement schedule issue hoga.

## 3. Documents Required For Car Insurance Transfer

Insurance transfer ke liye aapko in documents ki zaroorat hoti hai:

### Buyer Ke Documents:
1. **Transferred RC (Smart Card Photo):** Agar RTO se smart card aane mein time hai, to RTO ka **Form 29 & Form 30 acknowledgement receipt** bhi valid hota hai.
2. **Buyer KYC:** Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, aur Passport-size Photo.
3. **Insurance Transfer Form:** Insurer ka formal application form jisme buyer ke signature hon.

### Seller Ke Documents:
1. **Old Insurance Policy Copy:** Existing valid policy schedule.
2. **Sale Deed / Delivery Challan:** Car handover receipt.
3. **Seller Consent / NOC Letter:** Ek simple letter jisme seller likhta hai ki usne gaadi bech di hai aur insurance transfer par koi objection nahi hai.

## Car Insurance Transfer — Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal (FAQ)

### Sawaal: Can car insurance be transferred to a new owner?
Haan, bilkul! Motor Vehicles Act Section 157 ke tehat car insurance naye maalik ke naam transfer kiya ja sakta hai. Iske liye gaadi ki RC transfer receipt, seller ka NOC, aur buyer ke KYC documents ki zaroorat hoti hai.

### Sawaal: Car insurance transfer karne ki time limit kitni hoti hai?
Gaadi ki ownership transfer hone ki date se **exact 14 calendar days** ke andar insurance policy transfer ke liye apply karna kanoonan anivarya hai.

### Sawaal: Agar 14 din ke andar insurance transfer na karwayein to kya hoga?
Agar 14 din ke baad koi accident hota hai, to insurance company **Own Damage claim seedha reject kar sakti hai** kyunki policy holder aur RC owner ka naam match nahi karega. Iske alawa road par bina valid insurance maane jaane par ₹2,000 ka traffic challan bhi lag sakta hai.

### Sawaal: Kya car insurance ka NCB naye owner ko milta hai?
Nahi. No Claim Bonus (NCB) safe driving record ka reward hai jo seller (purane maalik) ko belong karta hai. Naye owner ko policy ke remaining tenure ka pro-rata NCB discount insurance company ko pay karna hota hai.

### Sawaal: Car insurance transfer charges kitne hote hain?
India Motor Tariff guidelines ke anusaar, standard insurance transfer endorsement fee sirf **₹50 + 18% GST (approx ₹59)** hoti hai. Iske alawa agar purani policy par NCB discount tha, to wo difference amount pay karna padta hai.

### Sawaal: Kya new RC aane se pehle insurance transfer ho sakta hai?
Haan! Agar RTO smart card aane mein time lag raha hai, to aap RTO ki official **Form 29 & Form 30 submission acknowledgement slip** ke basis par bhi insurance transfer endorsement karwa sakte hain.
