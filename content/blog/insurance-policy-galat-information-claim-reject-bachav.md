## 1. Top 4 Galatiyan Jinki Wajah Se Insurance Claim Reject Hota Hai

### 1. Wrong No Claim Bonus (NCB) Fill Karna
Sabse bada fraud flag NCB ka hota hai. Agar aapne pichle saal claim liya tha lekin naye portal par discount paane ke chakkar mein ya galti se 20% ya 50% NCB select kar diya, toh insurance company claim ke time pichli company se verification mangti hai. Agar mismatch mila, toh **Section 64VB ke tehat aapka poora claim cancel** ho jata hai.

### 2. Engine Number ya Chassis Number Typo
Ek bhi digit ya letter galat type ho gaya (jaise '0' ki jagah 'O' ya '8' ki jagah 'B'), toh surveyor inspection ke time gaadi ke chassis stamp se policy match nahi hogi. Is mismatch ko theek karwane mein hafton ka surveyor correspondence lag jata hai.

### 3. Galat Variant / Fuel Type (CNG Not Endorsed)
Agar aapki car Swift VXI hai aur aapne form mein LXI select kar diya, ya aftermarket CNG kit lagwayi hai par policy mein endorse nahi karwaya, toh engine aur electrical fire ka claim company turant reject kar deti hai.

### 4. Previous Policy Number Mismatch
Break-in policy mein agar purani policy ki details galat enter ho gayi, toh continuous coverage break maani jaati hai aur inspection mandatory ho jaati hai.
