## 1. Showroom / Dealer Renewal Itna Mehnga Kyun Hota Hai?

Jab aap Meerut ke kisi authorized showroom se renewal quote lete hain, toh unke quote mein ye additional factors jude hote hain:

1. **Unnecessary Add-ons:** Tyre cover, key replacement, ya engine protect un gaadiyon mein bhi add kar diye jaate hain jinki zaroorat nahi hoti.
2. **High Dealer Commission:** Showroom payouts kaafi high hote hain jiska bojh seedha policyholder ke premium par padta hai.
3. **No Claim Bonus (NCB) Neglect:** Agar aapne pichle saal koi claim nahi liya, toh aapka 20% se 50% NCB banta hai. Dealer forms mein aksar is discount ko properly pass nahi kiya jata.

## Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal (FAQ)

### Sawaal: Agar main showroom ke bahar se insurance renew karun toh kya cashless repair milega?
Haan, bilkul. Insurance policy ICICI Lombard, Tata AIG ya HDFC ERGO jaisi leading company ki hoti hai jinka tie-up Meerut ke sabhi Maruti Arena aur Nexa workshops ke sath direct hota hai. Isliye aapko poora cashless benefit milta hai.

### Sawaal: Maruti Swift ya Baleno par Zero Dep lena zaroori hai?
Agar aapki Maruti car 5 saal se kam purani hai, toh Zero Depreciation (Bumper to Bumper) add-on lena sabse samajhdari hai. Maruti cars ke fiber bumpers aur headlights par standard policy mein 50% deduction hota hai, jabki Zero Dep mein 100% paisa insurance company deti hai.

### Sawaal: Pichle saal policy expire ho gayi thi, kya Maruti ka No Claim Bonus bach sakta hai?
Agar aapki Maruti car ki policy expire hue 90 din se kam time hua hai, toh aapka poora No Claim Bonus (NCB 20% se 50%) safe rehta hai. SafeDrive par hum break-in self-inspection link provide karte hain jisse car ghar baithe 1 ghante mein reinspect ho jaati hai.
