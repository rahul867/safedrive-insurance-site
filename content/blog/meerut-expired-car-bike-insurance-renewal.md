## 1. UP-15 Traffic Police Fine vs Insurance Premium

Motor Vehicles Amendment Act ke Section 196 ke tehat:
* **Pehli baar pakde gaye:** ₹2,000 ka court challan.
* **Dusri baar pakde gaye:** ₹4,000 ka fine.

Dhyan rahe ki 100cc-150cc bike (Splendor, Platina, Pulsar) ka pure 1 saal ka Third Party Insurance sirf **₹843** mein ho jaata hai. Yaani ek challan ke kharche mein aap 2 saal se zyada time tak bina kisi chinta ke gaadi chala sakte hain.

## 2. 90 Din Ka Golden Rule: NCB Bachaiye

No Claim Bonus (NCB) wo discount hai jo pichhle saal claim na lene par 20% se lekar 50% tak milta hai:
* **Expiry ke 90 din ke andar:** Agar aap 90 din ke andar policy renew karwa lete hain, to aapka poora **NCB discount 100% safe** rehta hai.
* **90 din nikalne ke baad:** NCB zero ho jaata hai aur agle saal ka premium 20% se 50% extra mehnga ho jaata hai.
