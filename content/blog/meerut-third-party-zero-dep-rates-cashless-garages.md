## 1. Meerut Rate Chart: Kaunsa Plan Kitne Ka Padega?

* **Third Party (75cc se 150cc - Splendor, Pulsar, Activa):** Government fixed rate lagbhag **₹843 (GST sahit)**. Traffic challan se bachne ke liye best.

* **Zero Depreciation (Bumper to Bumper):** Plastic aur fiber bumper par **0% katauti**. Nayi Swift, Creta, Brezza, Nexon ke liye 100% zaroori.
