## 1. Third Party Bike Insurance Kya Hota Hai?

Third Party insurance ka matlab hai:
* **First Party:** Aap (Vehicle Owner)
* **Second Party:** Insurance Company
* **Third Party:** Road par koi bhi anjaan vyakti, uski gaadi ya property jisko aapki bike se nuksan pahuche.

Agar aapki bike se kisi dusri gaadi ko takkar lagti hai ya kisi vyakti ko injury aati hai, to kanoon ke tehat saara compensation insurance company bharti hai. Dhyan rahe, **Third Party policy mein aapki apni bike ka damage cover nahi hota** (apni bike ke cover ke liye Comprehensive policy leni hoti hai).

## Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal (FAQ)

### Sawaal: Third party bike insurance lena compulsory kyu hai?
Motor Vehicles Act 1988 aur 2019 amendment ke tehat Bharat ki kisi bhi public road par bike chalane ke liye kam se kam Third Party Insurance hona kanooni roop se anivarya (compulsory) hai.

### Sawaal: Kya Third Party policy lene ke liye bike ka inspection karwana padta hai?
Nahi! Agar aap sirf Third Party insurance le rahe hain, to chahe aapki bike kitne bhi din se expired kyun na ho, kisi bhi tarah ke inspection ya video verification ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Ye turant issue ho jaati hai.

### Sawaal: Kya Third Party insurance mein chori (Theft) cover hota hai?
Nahi. Third Party insurance mein sirf saamne wale vyakti ya gaadi ka nuksan cover hota hai. Bike chori ya khud ke accident damage ke liye aapko Comprehensive ya Standalone Own Damage (OD) cover lena zaroori hai.
