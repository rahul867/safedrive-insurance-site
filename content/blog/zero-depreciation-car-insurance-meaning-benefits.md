Car khareedte waqt har koi bolta hai — *"Bhaiya, Zero Dep policy lena!"* Lekin aamtaur par logon ko pata nahi hota ki Zero Depreciation (jise bolchaal mein Bumper-to-Bumper ya Nil Depreciation bhi kehte hain) normal comprehensive insurance se alag kaise hai aur claim ke time jeb se kitna paisa bachta hai.

Is guide mein hum aapko aasaan bhasha mein samjhayenge ki Zero Dep ka exact matlab kya hota hai, standard policy mein kitni katauti hoti hai, aur ye kiske liye lena compulsory hai.

---

## 1. Depreciation Kya Hai Aur Claim Me Katauti Kaise Hoti Hai?

Jaise hi aapki car showroom se bahar aati hai, uske parts ki value waqt ke sath kam hone lagti hai — ise **Depreciation** kehte hain.

Agar aapke paas **Standard Comprehensive Policy** hai aur gaadi ka accident ho jaata hai, to insurance company parts ke replacement par depreciation kaat leti hai:

| Material Type / Part | Standard Policy Me Depreciation Katauti | Zero Dep Policy Me Katauti |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rubber, Nylon, Plastic Parts** (Bumper, Headlights, Dash) | **50% deduction** (Aadhe paise aapko dene padenge) | **0% deduction (Full claim paid)** |
| **Fiber Glass Components** | **30% deduction** | **0% deduction (Full claim paid)** |
| **Glass Parts** (Windshield, Window glass) | **0% deduction** | **0% deduction (Full claim paid)** |
| **Metal Parts** (Doors, Bonnet, Dikki) | **Age ke hisab se 0% se 50% deduction** | **0% deduction (Full claim paid)** |
| **Paint Job** | **Standard table ke tehat 20-50% cut** | **Full claim covered** |

## 2. Real Example: Standard vs Zero Dep Claim Payout

Maan lijiye aapki Maruti Swift ya Hyundai Creta ka aage ka fiber bumper, headlights aur fender damage ho gaye:

* **Workshop Repair Bill:** ₹30,000
  * Plastic Bumper & Headlights: ₹20,000
  * Metal Fender & Paint: ₹10,000
* **Agar Standard Policy Hai:**
  * Plastic par 50% cut = ₹10,000 aapki jeb se
  * Metal/Paint cut + Compulsory deductible = lagbhag ₹4,000 aapki jeb se
  * **Insurance Company degi:** ₹16,000 | **Aapko dena padega:** ₹14,000!
* **Agar Zero Dep Policy Hai:**
  * Depreciation deduction = ₹0
  * **Insurance Company degi:** ₹28,500 | **Aapko dena padega:** Sirf standard file charge (lagbhag ₹1,000 se ₹1,500).

## 3. Zero Dep Policy Kisko Leni Chahiye?

SafeDrive ke motor advisors in 4 categories ke vehicle owners ko Zero Dep lene ki strong recommendation dete hain:

1. **Nayi Gaadiyan (0 se 5 Saal Purani):** Nayi gaadi ke parts mehenge hote hain. Zero Dep lene se car bilkul showroom jaisi secure rehti hai.
2. **First-Time Drivers:** Agar aapne nayi car chalana shuru kiya hai, to minor bumps aur scratches aana aam baat hai.
3. **Luxury & Premium Cars:** Audi, BMW, Mercedes, ya top-end SUVs (Fortuner, XUV700, Safari) jinke plastic sensors aur LED matrix lights laakhon mein aate hain.
4. **Heavy Traffic Commuters (Gurgaon, Delhi, Noida):** Highway aur congested road commute mein bumper hit hona daily risk hai.

---

## 4. Zero Dep Policy Me Kya Cover Nahi Hota?

Kuch specific items Zero Dep mein bhi include nahi hote:
* Compulsory Deductible (IRDAI mandated ₹1,000 se ₹2,000 standard file charge).
* Mechanical / Electrical breakdown bina kisi accident ke.
* Normal wear and tear ya tyres/tubes ka ghisaav (jab tak accident na hua ho).
* Agar gaadi drink and drive mein chalayi ja rahi thi.

## Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal (FAQ)

### Sawaal: Zero Dep car insurance ka matlab kya hota hai?
Zero Depreciation (Nil Dep ya Bumper to Bumper) car insurance ka wo comprehensive cover hai jisme claim ke time plastic, fiber, rubber aur metal parts par koi depreciation katauti nahi hoti aur insurance company 100% replacement kharcha bear karti hai.

### Sawaal: Zero Dep policy standard policy se kitni mehengi hoti hai?
Aamtaur par Zero Depreciation add-on normal comprehensive insurance ke premium se lagbhag 15% se 20% zyada hota hai. Lekin ek hi claim mein ye extra premium se kayi guna zyada savings karwa deta hai.

### Sawaal: Ek saal mein kitne Zero Dep claims le sakte hain?
Zyadatar companies (Tata AIG, ICICI Lombard, HDFC ERGO) ek policy period (1 saal) mein 2 Zero Dep claims allow karti hain. Kuch select policies unlimited Zero Dep claims ka option bhi deti hain.
